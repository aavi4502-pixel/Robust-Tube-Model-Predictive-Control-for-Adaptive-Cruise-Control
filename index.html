Core Theoretical Architecture

The four pillars of the proposed Tube MPC design.

1. Instantaneous Step-0 Conditioning (Theorem 2)

Standard tube MPC computes nominal input u_xi and adds ancillary feedback c_t outside the solver, causing the total applied input u_app = u_xi + c_t to violate slew limits. Our architecture directly constrains the first decision u_xi(0|t) against u_app(t-1) and instantaneous c_t, mathematically proving |u_app(t) - u_app(t-1)| ≤ Δu_max.

2. Trajectory-Dependent Uncertainty Propagation

Rather than imposing static worst-case bounds across all horizon steps, ellipsoidal disturbance bounds propagate recursively along nominal state transitions under zero-order hold powertrain lag, reducing peak uncertainty sigma_peak from 22.15 to 0.39 and preventing input-interval collapse.

3. LMI-Verified Ancillary Feedback Selection

The ancillary gain K_opt = [1.2345, -0.5985, -1.2588] is synthesized via discrete Lyapunov dissipation LMIs with R=50. It minimizes input support coefficient g_u = 0.755533, expanding collapse capacity by +21.66% and reducing control total variation by 48.4%.

4. Slew-Invariant Fallback Execution (Proposition 2)

When aggressive headway deficits make nominal optimization infeasible, a deterministic rate-limited fallback controller executes monotonic deceleration at exactly -Δu_max down to u_min, guaranteeing inductive rate compliance across all switching sequences.
